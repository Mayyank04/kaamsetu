## 1. Executive Charter

KAAMSETU's trust governance framework ensures that informal blue-collar workers, contractors, and institutions operate within a fair, transparent, and enforceable environment.

### The Five Inviolable Trust Principles:
1. **Dignity of Labor:** Every craftsperson is entitled to a safe work site, timely statutory compensation, and professional respect.
2. **0.00% Wage Exploitation:** The platform will never extract commissions or booking cuts from worker wages.
3. **Whistleblower Immunity:** No worker may be penalized, algorithmically suppressed, or retaliated against for reporting safety or wage violations.
4. **Preservation of Identity:** Worker reputation, verified credentials, and portfolios are permanently owned by the worker, unaffected by contractor account status.
5. **Radical Audit Transparency:** All disputes, sanctions, and credential revocations must maintain an immutable cryptographic audit trail.

## 3. Contractor Code of Conduct & Restitution Rules

Contractors using KAAMSETU agree to:
1. Disburse full agreed compensation within 24 hours of job completion.
2. Provide mandatory on-site personal protective equipment (PPE) for hazardous trades (welding, electrical, roofing).
3. Refrain from retaining physical identity documents (Aadhaar cards, voter IDs).

### Restitution & Sanctions Ladder:
- **First Violation (Wage Withholding):** Immediate account restriction + mandatory payment of full wage difference within 48 hours.
- **Second Violation (Repeated Withholding):** 30-day suspension from opportunity posting + public notation on contractor profile.
- **Third Violation / Physical Abuse:** Permanent ban from the platform + referral to regional labor commissioner authorities.

## 4. Evidence Vault & Dispute Immutability

Under `public.grievance_evidence_vault`:
- Evidence files (photographs of completed work, WhatsApp screenshots, bank transfers) are stored in isolated encrypted storage buckets.
- Evidence cannot be deleted or modified by either party during an active dispute.
- Storage policies grant read access only to the assigned Field Coordinator and platform administrators.
