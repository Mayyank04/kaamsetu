## 3. ANTI-SLOP VERIFICATION GATE

Future modifications must pass these 5 gates before merge:
1. **Zero Radial Blur Circles:** No `blur-lg` or `backdrop-blur-md` without approved physical necessity.
2. **Zero Fabricated Social Proof:** No unverified user counts, star ratings, or corporate partner logos.
3. **Zero Decorative Icon Clutter:** Every icon must serve navigation, orientation, or status clarity.
4. **Zero Unearned Verified Badges:** Green verified status is reserved for cryptographic/institutional proofs.
5. **Humanized Tone:** Zero chatbot marketing phrases ("revolutionary", "all-in-one ecosystem").
