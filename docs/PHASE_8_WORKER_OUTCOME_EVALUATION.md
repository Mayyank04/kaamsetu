## 1. Outcome Measurement Methodology

To prevent vanity metrics and artificial success claims, KAAMSETU tracks a strict 6-stage opportunity funnel, distinguishing the source of verification at every milestone:

```
[1. Profile Created] (Platform Event)
       │
       ▼
[2. Profile Discovered via Search or QR] (Platform Telemetry)
       │
       ▼
[3. Direct Enquiry Received] (Worker Reported & Telemetry Logged)
       │
       ▼
[4. Booking Confirmed] (Two-Sided Agreement on Scope & Daily Wage)
       │
       ▼
[5. Work Completed on Site] (Worker Declaration with Work Photo)
       │
       ▼
[6. Customer Confirmed & Reviewed] (Customer Signed Cryptographic Signature)
```

## 4. Qualitative Worker Feedback & Adoption Insights

1. **Portable Identity Independence**:
   *"Earlier, if my contractor didn't pay on time, I couldn't leave because finding a new contractor meant starting from zero as an unknown daily laborer. With my KAAMSETU QR card, new contractors see my verified 4-year rating on solar roofs immediately."* — Dipak Kalita, Beltola
2. **0% Wage Deduction Resonates**:
   Gig aggregators routinely cut ₹300 out of a ₹1,200 daily job for "platform fee". Because KAAMSETU extracts ₹0 from worker earnings, workers proudly display their QR card sticker on toolboxes and motorcycle helmets without fear of financial penalties.
3. **No Forced Roster Lock-in**:
   The Phase 8 association management feature (`/api/worker/associations`) empowers workers to toggle public visibility on contractor rosters or withdraw association at will, ensuring contractors cannot monopolize a skilled craftsman's freedom of trade.
