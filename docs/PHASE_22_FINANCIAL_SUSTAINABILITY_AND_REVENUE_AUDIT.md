### 1. The Ethical Business Model

KAAMSETU's financial viability does NOT depend on extracting fees from impoverished workers. ### 3. The 10-State Commercial Billing Lifecycle

To prevent premature revenue recognition, B2B SaaS contracts progress through 10 formal accounting states:
1. `quote_drafted`
2. `proforma_issued`
3. `po_received`
4. `tax_invoice_generated`
5. `payment_pending`
6. `bank_transfer_verified`
7. `gst_liability_booked`
8. `revenue_recognized`
9. `closed`
10. `reconciled`

#### Revenue Segregation Principles
- **Cash Collected $\ne$ Invoiced ARR $\ne$ Recognized Revenue**: Annual software licenses (₹1,50,000/yr) are recognized monthly ($\frac{1}{12}\text{th}$ per month) in compliance with Ind AS 115 (Revenue from Contracts with Customers).
- **Zero Float Risk**: Worker wages never touch platform bank accounts. There is zero commingling of customer service payments with company operating capital.

### 4. Unit Economics & Operating Margin

- **Cost of Software Operations (COGS):** Cloud hosting (Supabase / PostgreSQL, Vercel edge), SMS OTP gateways, and encrypted storage consume ₹1,85,000 per annum across active regions.
- **Gross Software Margin:**
  $$\mathbf{Gross\text{ }Margin} = \frac{₹4,50,000 - ₹1,85,000}{₹4,50,000} = \mathbf{58.89\%} \approx \mathbf{58.9\%}$$
- **Sustainability Verdict:** With healthy gross margins on software licensing, KAAMSETU achieves operating sustainability without charging informal workers a single rupee.
