### 1. The Enterprise Pilot Integrity Problem

In enterprise staffing and workforce software, vendors routinely present signed pilot letters or imported employee spreadsheets as "enterprise deployments."

KAAMSETU enforces the **Enterprise Anti-Conflation Framework**:
1. An imported enterprise roster does NOT constitute a worker deployment.
2. A contractor's daily work report does NOT constitute verified work performed.
3. An internal contractor accounting debit does NOT constitute worker wage payment.

---

### 2. The 11-Stage Enterprise Pilot Lifecycle

Every enterprise engagement (such as the NHIDCL Highway Expansion pilot and Shillong Airport civil maintenance trial) must progress linearly through 11 audited stages:

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    E1[1. Lead] --> E2[2. NDA]
    E2 --> E3[3. Workshop]
    E3 --> E4[4. Draft MoU]
    E4 --> E5[5. Signed MoU]
    E5 --> E6[6. Consented Cohort]
    E6 --> E7[7. Geofenced Site]
    E7 --> E8[8. Work Executed]
    E8 --> E9[9. Disbursed UTR]
    E9 --> E10[10. Audit Signoff]
    E10 --> E11[11. Commercial Conversion]
```

#### Detailed Stage Specifications
1. **`lead_identified`**: Initial enterprise enquiry or RFP response recorded.
2. **`nda_executed`**: Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement bilaterally signed.
3. **`scoping_workshop`**: Technical site evaluation, safety requirements, and wage benchmarks established.
4. **`pilot_mou_drafted`**: Scope, cohort size, and duration documented in standard legal template.
5. **`pilot_mou_signed`**: Corporate board resolution or Power of Attorney verified before signature.
6. **`worker_cohort_consented`**: Each worker in the cohort individually reviews and grants DPDP consent for project assignment.
7. **`geofenced_telematics_active`**: Physical perimeter coordinates registered with GPS radius validation (< 500m).
8. **`work_executed`**: Daily shift completions logged with biometric or supervisor OTP verification.
9. **`wage_disbursed_utr`**: Contractor uploads banking UTRs proving direct bank transfer of wages to workers.
10. **`audit_signoff`**: Independent field auditor verifies wage receipt directly with a sample of workers.
11. **`commercial_conversion`**: Transition to paid enterprise SaaS annual licensing subscription.

### 3. Anti-Conflation Enforcement Rules

| Claim Attempt | Verification Rule | System Enforcement Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Claiming Deployment from Roster Import** | Physical geofence check-in event required | Stage 7 remains locked; workers marked `roster_only` |
| **Claiming Work Done from Contractor Report** | Worker check-out OTP + supervisor sign-off | Stage 8 rejected without dual-attestation tokens |
| **Claiming Wage Payment from Contractor Word** | Validated banking UTR regex required | Stage 9 strictly blocked; pilot cannot advance |
| **Claiming Commercial Revenue from MoU** | Stamped contract + paid invoice receipt | Accounting distinguishes `pipeline` from `revenue` |

### 4. Government e-Marketplace (GeM) Form B Compliance

For public infrastructure and EPC contractor engagements:
- Invoicing follows **GeM Contract Form B** standards.
- Invoices clearly separate:
  1. Software platform licensing / workforce management SaaS fee (taxable at 18% GST).
  2. Worker wage component (transferred 100% directly from contractor to worker accounts without touching platform books).
- **Constitutional Invariant:** In no circumstance does the platform deduct or invoice a percentage fee against worker wages.
