### 3. Occupational Safety & Fair Marketplace Protections

1. **Zero Critical Safety Incidents:**
   - Across all active regional jobs, **exactly 0 fatal, severe, or hospitalization-grade occupational injuries** were reported.
   - Every high-risk trade onboarding enforces basic PPE verification (safety goggles, dust masks, steel-toed boots).

2. **Grievance Resolution Desk SLA:**
   - **Service Level Agreement:** 100% of grievances resolved within 48.0 hours.
   - **Observed Resolution Latency:** **18.4 Hours** average turnaround time.
   - **Outcome Breakdown:** 82% amicable resolution, 14% mediated settlement, 4% dismissed as non-actionable.

3. **Anti-Retaliation Moderation Shielding:**
   - Customer ratings submitted while a payment dispute or wage claim is actively open are automatically quarantined in moderation.
   - If an employer or contractor attempts to use a 1-star review as leverage to reduce wages, the review is expunged by the neutral Ombudsman.
