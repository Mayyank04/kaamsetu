## 3. Key Findings & Strategic Insights for Phase 7 Commercial Product

1. **Top Conversion Driver: One-Click Client Compliance Dossier (PDF)**:
   16 of 18 contractors highlighted that residential and commercial clients increasingly demand proof of identity and technical competence. The ability to export a co-branded PDF dossier for a 5-to-25 person site crew is the #1 feature justifying payment.
2. **Top Retention Driver: 0% Worker Extraction (Zero Labor Resentment)**:
   Contractors explicitly praised the 0% worker commission invariant. In gig platforms, workers actively attempt to bypass the platform because 20% is subtracted from their pay. Because KAAMSETU costs workers ₹0, workers take pride in their digital card and willingly carry it on site.
3. **Payment Preference: UPI & Bank QR over Credit Cards**:
   82% of regional contractors in Assam pay business vendors via UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, BHIM) or direct NEFT/RTGS with a GST invoice, rather than auto-debit credit cards. The billing engine must prioritize UPI Intent and QR payment flows.
