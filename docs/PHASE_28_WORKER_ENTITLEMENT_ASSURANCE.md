# KAAMSETU — PHASE 28 WORKER ENTITLEMENT ASSURANCE
## Transparent Statements, Statutory Minimum Wage Compliance & Direct Pass-Through

**Evaluation Context:** December 2026  
**Auditor:** Worker Protection Lead & Regional Operations Controller

---

## 1. Principles of Worker Entitlement

Informal and gig workers frequently suffer from opaque earnings calculations, hidden deductions, and non-transparent penalty schemes. KAAMSETU guarantees:
1. **Total Transparency:** Every worker has real-time access to transparent wage statements displaying agreed wage, material reimbursements, and zero platform deductions.
2. **Statutory Minimum Wage Premium:** Wage rates on KAAMSETU must meet or exceed state statutory minimum wages for skilled trades.
3. **No Hidden Platform Retentions:** Direct 100% pass-through from customer payment to artisan account.

---

## 2. Transparent Wage Statement Structure

As tested in [TEST 31] and [TEST 32] of `test:phase28`, worker wage statements provide explicit line items:
- **Gross Agreed Customer Payment:** ₹3,600.00
- **Direct Material Reimbursement (if applicable):** ₹0.00
- **Platform Commission:** **₹0.00 (0.00%)**
- **Net Worker Entitlement:** **₹3,600.00**
- **Settlement Method:** IMPS/UPI Direct to Bank
- **Bank Reference:** Masked (e.g., `UTR202610AS20001` -> `XXXX-XXXX-0001`) to protect artisan privacy while preserving auditability.
- **Dispute Flag:** Clearly visible button allowing instant ombudsperson escalation within 7 calendar days.
