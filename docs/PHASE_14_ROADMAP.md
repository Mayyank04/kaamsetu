### 2. Strategic Pillars & Workstreams for Phase 14

#### Workstream 1: Activating the Three Candidate Expansion Corridors
1. **West Tripura (`IN-TR-WTR`, Agartala Hub)**:
   - Finalize bilateral MoU with Tripura State Skill Development Mission (TSSDM).
   - Complete Milestone 03 through 09 of the State Launch Playbook.
   - Onboard initial cohort of 30 construction craftspeople and bamboo artisans.
   - Target Activation: Q1 2027.
2. **North Bengal Corridor (`IN-WB-DAR`, Siliguri & Darjeeling Transit Hub)**:
   - Execute baseline labor market survey across tea estate workers and municipal construction crews.
   - Deploy Bengali (`bn`) and Nepali (`ne`) voice localization packs with zero-hallucination guarantees.
   - Target Soft-Launch: Q2 2027.
3. **Nagaland Commercial Gateway (`IN-NL-DIM`, Dimapur Hub)**:
   - Appoint Nagaland regional operations lead.
   - Map inter-state transit corridors between Dimapur and Upper Assam (Golaghat / Jorhat).
   - Target Soft-Launch: Q2 2027.

---

#### Workstream 2: National Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) Integration
1. **National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) & Skill India Digital (SID)**:
   - Implement bi-directional API gateway with NSDC's Skill India Digital portal.
   - Enable direct ingestion of National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS) records.
   - Map all KAAMSETU trade competencies to the National Occupational Standards (NOS).
2. **DigiLocker W3C Verifiable Credential Issuer**:
   - Complete formal NeGD institutional onboarding agreement.
   - Enable workers to pull their KAAMSETU Schema 2.2 skill card directly into their government DigiLocker app as an official verifiable document.
3. **e-Shram Portal Interoperability**:
   - Establish voluntary linkage with the Ministry of Labour & Employment's e-Shram database, allowing unorganized workers to prove social security enrollment without redundant paperwork.

---

#### Workstream 3: Offline Bluetooth Mesh & Audio QR Verification
For worksites with zero mobile connectivity (tunnel construction, remote hydroelectric dams, offshore barges):
1. **BLE Offline Handshake**:
   - Implement Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) peer-to-peer credential exchange between worker card/app and contractor verification scanner without internet access.
2. **Audio-Tone QR Verification**:
   - Introduce near-ultrasonic sound-wave data exchange for feature phone users without camera access.
3. **Decentralized Revocation Bloom Filters**:
   - Distribute compact cryptographic Bloom filters of revoked credentials to mobile scanner apps during nightly Wi-Fi syncs, enabling instant offline fraud rejection.

---

#### Workstream 4: Enterprise Contractor Integrations (ERP Connectors)
1. **Enterprise Construction Management Connectors**:
   - Pre-built webhooks and data connectors for Procore, SAP S/4HANA Construction, and Autodesk Construction Cloud.
2. **Automated Site Access Gate Integration**:
   - RFID / QR turnstile integration allowing workers to tap their physical KAAMSETU card at construction site entry gates for instantaneous credential and safety gear verification.
3. **Statutory Labor Law Compliance Engine**:
   - Automated generation of Form D (Equal Remuneration Act) and Form XII (Contract Labour Act) compliance registers.

---

#### Workstream 5: Worker Welfare Micro-Insurance & Benefits Portability
1. **Voluntary Workplace Accidental Micro-Cover**:
   - Partner with public-sector insurance carriers to offer optional, contractor-sponsored accidental disability and death micro-insurance (₹200,000 cover for ₹12/month).
   - 100% voluntary, zero payroll deductions from workers.
2. **Portable Health Record Storage**:
   - Enable workers to store tetanus vaccination records and occupational health fitness certificates in their Schema 2.2 dossier.
