### 1. Commercial Foundation & The Zero-Commission Model

KAAMSETU demonstrates that a digital labor platform can achieve robust commercial viability, high gross margins, and institutional scalability **without taxing the wages of vulnerable workers**.

By refusing to extract commissions from informal laborers, KAAMSETU builds deep artisan trust, driving organic word-of-mouth onboarding and achieving near-zero worker acquisition costs (CAC $\approx ₹42$ per verified artisan).

Commercial revenue is generated strictly from **B2B Enterprise Subscriptions**, **Contractor Pro SaaS tiers**, and **Institutional Verification Licenses**.

### 3. Ethical NBFC Tool-Financing Partnership Framework

Skilled informal artisans (e.g. carpenters, electricians, fabricators) often face severe income caps due to a lack of modern power tools (circular saws, rotary hammer drills, inverter welding units).

KAAMSETU establishes an institutional framework for **Ethical NBFC Tool Financing**, governed by strict worker-protection invariants:

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                     THE 6 INVIOLABLE RESPONSIBLE FINANCE PILLARS              │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. RBI-Regulated Entities Only: Zero unregulated digital lending apps.        │
│ 2. APR Interest Rate Ceiling: Maximum APR capped at <= 14.0% per annum.       │
│ 3. Strict Wage Lien Prohibition: EMIs can NEVER be deducted from worker wages.│
│ 4. 15-Day Unconditional Return: Full refund and cancellation without penalty. │
│ 5. Bundled Equipment Insurance: Mandatory coverage for theft, fire & damage.  │
│ 6. Zero Pre-Payment Penalty: Artisans may foreclose loans early at ₹0 cost.   │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Detailed Ethical Invariants

1. **Wage Lien Prohibition (Architectural Invariant):**
   - Traditional platform micro-financing deducts loan payments directly from daily gig earnings before the worker receives a rupee.
   - In KAAMSETU, **wage disbursement and loan repayment are strictly air-gapped**.
   - Artisans receive 100% of their wages into their personal accounts. They pay tool loan EMIs separately via NACH/e-mandate or UPI directly to the lender.
2. **Statutory Regulatory Disclaimer:**
   - **KAAMSETU IS NEITHER A BANK, A NON-BANKING FINANCIAL COMPANY (NBFC), A LENDER, NOR A CREDIT RATING AGENCY.**
   - KAAMSETU does not originate loans, assume balance-sheet credit risk, or perform coercive debt recovery.
   - KAAMSETU acts solely as a technological facilitator sharing verified vocational credentials upon explicit worker consent.
