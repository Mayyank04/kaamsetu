# KAAMSETU — UNVERIFIED CLAIMS & SYSTEM LIMITATIONS
## Explicit Scope Limitations, Partner-Unverified Dependencies & Epistemological Boundaries

**Governing Epistemological Principle:**
> *"A hash proves that evidence has not changed since hashing. It does not prove that the underlying claim is true. A test proves only what that test actually tests. A database row does not prove a real-world event."*

---

### 1. Purpose of this Document

In institutional software engineering, the greatest failure mode is **over-claiming technical readiness as institutional reality**. This document provides an exhaustive, unsparing inventory of what KAAMSETU's software does **not** prove, what remains unverified by third-party counterparties, and the boundaries of current system knowledge.

---

### 2. Comprehensive Inventory of Unverified Claims & System Limitations

#### A. Enterprise Pilots (Assam & Meghalaya EPC Projects)
- **What is Verified:** The software successfully parses SAP SuccessFactors OData v4 schemas, builds GeM Form B compliance reports, and tracks 0% commission wage disbursement records in synthetic test environments.
- **What is UNVERIFIED:** No executed contract on non-judicial stamp paper has been signed by authorized corporate officers of the candidate EPC enterprises.
- **Limitation:** The platform cannot legally deploy workers to these enterprise sites until physical agreements are signed.

#### B. ONDC BPP & ONest / DSEP Network Interoperability
- **What is Verified:** All 10 staging protocol tests pass; Beckn search/order callbacks conform to specification; Ed25519 signing ceremonies operate without error.
- **What is UNVERIFIED:** The ONDC Registry Council has not issued live production whitelisting keys. The ONest consortium membership is pending live key ceremony.
- **Limitation:** Live consumer or government orders from the ONDC network cannot be routed into the KAAMSETU worker registry today.

#### C. Tool & Equipment Micro-Financing
- **What is Verified:** Software calculations enforce APR $\le 14.0\%$, compute 3-day cooling-off refunds, generate unbundled Key Fact Statements (KFS), and enforce 0% intermediary deductions.
- **What is UNVERIFIED:** The candidate NBFC has not signed the mandatory Tripartite LSP Agreement required under RBI September 2022 guidelines.
- **Limitation:** No informal worker can currently apply for or receive a live equipment loan through the platform.

#### D. Commercial Revenue & Financial Settlements
- **What is Verified:** B2B SaaS pricing tiers, 18% GST calculation, razorpay webhook signatures, and 10-state settlement progression are verified in code.
- **What is UNVERIFIED:** Commercial platform transactions currently reside in a simulated test ledger. No corporate bank account has cleared live merchant settlements.
- **Limitation:** KAAMSETU cannot claim realized operational corporate revenue until live merchant bank settlements occur.

#### E. Physical Trade Competence vs. Digital Credentials
- **What is Verified:** A cryptographic QR code or W3C Verifiable Credential proves that a digital record was signed by an authorized issuer key without subsequent tampering.
- **What is UNVERIFIED:** The QR code does **not** prove that an electrician knows how to wire a three-phase breaker box safely in the rain, nor does it guarantee the physical craftsmanship of a carpenter.
- **Limitation:** Digital credentials augment, but do not replace, hands-on trade testing and site supervisor verification.

#### F. Regional Gating Truth (Siliguri & Tripura)
- **What is Verified:** Siliguri achieves 85.7% quantitative readiness; Tripura achieves 57.1% quantitative readiness.
- **What is UNVERIFIED:** Siliguri lacks an executed physical field office lease. Tripura lacks an executed MoU with the Tripura Skill Development Mission.
- **Limitation:** Neither region can be marketed or operated as fully active.

---

### 3. Conclusion

KAAMSETU's technical architecture is world-class, but its operational claims are strictly bounded by verified legal and institutional reality. Controlled expansion under **Class B** is justified; claims of unconstrained Class A operations are rejected until external dependencies are satisfied.
