# KAAMSETU — PHASE 44: SMALL VENDOR & MICRO-ENTERPRISE EXPERIENCE
**Operating Classification:** `CLASS_B_CONTROLLED_ROLLOUT`  
**Permanent Financial Invariant:** `platform_commission_amount = 0.00` on artisan wages

---

## 1. Small Vendors as First-Class Citizens

In informal Indian labor markets, small vendors (vegetable and fruit sellers, street food carts, tea kiosks, tailors, cobblers, and micro-merchants) represent over 60% of independent livelihood earners. Previously, platforms treated them either as corporate delivery fleets or forced them into service worker molds with hourly/daily wage rates.

In Phase 44, KAAMSETU gives small vendors first-class product identity:
- **Profile Types:** `service_professional`, `goods_vendor`, `hybrid_artisan`.
- **Stall & Setup Classification:** Mobile Cart, Fixed Market Stall, Home Workshop, Street Kiosk, Retail Shop.
- **Operating Hours:** Dawn-to-dusk and split-shift schedule disclosures.
- **Product & Produce Catalog:** Granular itemized listings with transparent unit pricing (e.g., ₹20 / pair, ₹15 / cup, ₹60 / plate).
- **Zero Commission Guarantee:** Zero transaction fees or merchant commissions on vendor goods sales.

---

## 2. Vendor Journey & Workflow

1. **Voice or Text Onboarding:** Vendor speaks their local trade (e.g. "Main Paltan Bazar mein subah 6 baje se taazi sabziyon ka thela lagati hoon").
2. **Setup Customization:** In review screen, the vendor selects "Local Vendor / Merchant", enters their stall name, stall type, operating hours, and delivery preferences.
3. **Public Discovery Card:** Displayed on `/explore` with stall type badge, featured product pricing, and direct phone/in-person contact option.
4. **Product Catalog Manager:** In the vendor dashboard, items can be added, edited, and marked in-stock or out-of-stock.
