## 1. Finding Skilled Professionals & Vendors

The Customer Journey on KAAMSETU is built for speed, transparency, and trust:

1. **Filter by Role Type:** Customers can toggle between "All Listings", "Service Professionals", and "Local Vendors & Goods".
2. **Search by Trade or Item:** Search by craft ("Solar", "Joinery", "Plumbing"), vendor product ("Kaji Nemu", "Masala Chai", "Kurtas"), or city.
3. **Decoupled Geographic Exploration:** Filter across all 36 States and Union Territories and 700+ districts.
4. **Credential Verification Dialog:** Inspect verified skills, client ratings, peer references, and authentic KAAMSCORE™ breakdown before contacting.
5. **Commercial Dispatch:** In authorized pilot zones (Guwahati & Shillong), customers can request formal scheduled bookings. Outside pilot zones, customers contact professionals directly.
