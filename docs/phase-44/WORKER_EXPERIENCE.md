## 1. The 10-Section Guided Worker Workspace

The worker dashboard (`src/components/dashboard/worker-dashboard-container.tsx`) provides an integrated, dignified workspace for informal craftspeople:

1. **Dashboard Overview:** Actionable next steps, profile completeness tracker, quick performance index.
2. **Profile Information:** Complete trade profile, verifiable NCO code, operating location, contact preferences, and public link.
3. **My Skills & Competencies:** Granular sub-skills, equipment handling, and proof badges.
4. **Work Opportunities:** Direct customer inquiries and enterprise crew staffing invitations.
5. **Job Requests & Bookings:** Status-driven booking lifecycle (requested -> confirmed -> in_progress -> completed).
6. **Portfolio Showcase:** Visual photographic proof of real completed work.
7. **Credentials & KAAMSCORE:** Cryptographic proof, tamper-evident hash chain, DigiLocker verification status.
8. **Earnings & Settlement Ledger:** Permanent 0% platform commission ledger showing 100% net wage payouts.
9. **Notifications:** Real-time operational alerts for bookings, reviews, and crew invitations.
10. **Account & Safety Settings:** Language preferences, availability toggles, password management, and data export.
