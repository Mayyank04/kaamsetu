# KAAMSETU — PHASE 44: CONTRACTOR & INSTITUTION EXPERIENCE
**Operating Classification:** `CLASS_B_CONTROLLED_ROLLOUT`  
**Permanent Financial Invariant:** `platform_commission_amount = 0.00` on artisan wages

---

## 1. Contractor Pro Portal

Commercial contractors and project managers access the specialized Contractor Pro portal (`/contractor/dashboard`):
- **Organization Setup:** Manage enterprise profile, GSTIN, and commercial billing tier.
- **Projects & Opportunities:** Post commercial infrastructure and construction projects.
- **Workforce Matching:** Match qualified artisans based on verified sub-trades, tools handled, and location.
- **Worker Consent-First Invitations:** Workers must explicitly accept or reject crew invitations before contractors can assign them to project rosters.

---

## 2. Institutional Skill Registry

State skill development missions (ASDM, MSSDS), ITIs, and polytechnics utilize the Institutional Portal (`/institution/dashboard`):
- **Cohort Tracking:** Monitor candidate skill verification, peer endorsements, and transition into independent employment.
- **Credential Issuance:** Cryptographically anchor certified vocational badges to the public verification ledger.
