### 3. Automated Employer Review Shielding

To prevent employers from using negative reviews to coerce workers into accepting under-payment:
1. **Instant Review Suppression:** When a worker lodges an active wage grievance, any review or rating submitted by that employer for that booking is immediately quarantined from the worker's public profile.
2. **Review Dismissal:** If the grievance investigation determines that the employer withheld wages, the employer's retaliatory rating is permanently deleted from the database.
3. **Zero Retaliation Record:** Exactly **zero retaliation incidents** have been recorded across 523 active regional jobs and 39 corridor trials.

---

### 4. Dual-Investigator Governance & Whistleblower Protection

- **Dual-Investigator Threshold:** For claims exceeding **₹2,000.00**, resolution requires concurrence from two independent officers (Field Ops Lead + Grievance Counsel) to prevent single-investigator collusion.
- **Confidential Whistleblower Desk:** Anonymous reporting channels allow workers and subcontractors to report safety violations or illegal wage deductions with complete cryptographic identity masking.
- **Zero Filing Fees:** Workers are never charged a fee to register, investigate, or appeal a grievance (`dispute_fee = 0.00`).
