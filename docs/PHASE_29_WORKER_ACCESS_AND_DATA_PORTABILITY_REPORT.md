# KAAMSETU — PHASE 29 WORKER ACCESS & DATA PORTABILITY REPORT

### 1. DPDP Act 2023 Compliance
Under Section 6 and Section 8 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023:
- Workers have the right to complete data portability in open standard JSON format.
- Export includes worker profile, verified W3C credentials, and job transaction ledgers.
- Consent revocation latency conforms to SLA (< 500ms p99 measured).
