## 1. Enterprise Workforce Pilot Context

To protect unorganized construction workers, steel fixers, shuttering carpenters, and heavy equipment operators from wage theft and abusive contractor cartels, KAAMSETU structured an Enterprise Tripartite Operating Agreement.

Under this framework:
1. The Enterprise EPC Contractor pays software subscription fees (SaaS) to KAAMSETU.
2. The EPC Contractor deposits wage funds into an independent, non-lien escrow account.
3. Workers receive 100% of their statutory wages directly to their bank accounts with 0% platform commission.
4. Workers are covered by comprehensive worker injury insurance and statutory PF/ESIC compliance.
