# KAAMSETU — Customer Discovery & Validation Analysis (Guwahati Field Pilot)

**Testing Location:** Guwahati, Kamrup Metropolitan District, Assam  
**Customer Segments Tested:** Residential Homeowners (4), Apartment Society Committee Members (2), Local Retail Shopkeepers (2), Interior Renovation Contractors (2)  
**Total Customer Evaluators:** 10 Independent Evaluators  
**Evaluation Focus:** Discoverability, Trust Credibility, Profile Comprehension, Booking Friction

---

## 1. Executive Summary of Customer Research

Customer testing confirmed strong demand for an uncorrupted, evidence-backed directory of local trade professionals in Guwahati. Evaluators noted that traditional listing platforms (JustDial, Urban Company) often feature inflated reviews, high markups, or lack specialized informal trades (such as APDCL earthing electricians or bamboo joiners).

### Key Customer Perceptions
1. **Credibility of Evidence Separation**: Customers universally praised the visual separation between **Verified Evidence** (customer bookings, peer reviews) and **Self-Declared Skills**. They felt this honest distinction made KAAMSETU more trustworthy than platforms that award 5 stars to everyone.
2. **Value of Portfolio & Tool Details**: Seeing specific equipment (e.g. Megger insulation tester, CPVC fusion cutter) provided customers with immediate confidence that the worker was an experienced professional rather than an untrained amateur.
3. **QR Code Utility for On-Site Verification**: Apartment managers appreciated that an incoming electrician or carpenter could present a QR card at the security gate that instantly verifies their identity and trade background.
4. **Transparent Contracting Rates**: Customers appreciated seeing indicative daily rate ranges or "Consultation-Based" labels rather than misleading fixed flat fees that change upon arrival.
