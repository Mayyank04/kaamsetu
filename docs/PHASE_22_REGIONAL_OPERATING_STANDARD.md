### 1. The Repeatable Expansion Philosophy

A platform cannot scale informal workforce services through software code alone. Physical field trust, language inclusivity, district administration alignment, and rapid emergency dispute mechanisms are mandatory.

KAAMSETU establishes the **Standard Regional Operating Procedure (SROP)**: a formal 12-point readiness rubric that every new district must satisfy before initiating commercial operations.

---

### 2. The 12-Item SROP Evaluation Rubric

Every candidate district is evaluated across 12 rigorous operational checkpoints:

| Checkpoint # | Operational Requirement | Conformance Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SROP-01** | **District Labor Office Liaison** | Formal notification or liaison established with local District Labor Officer. |
| **SROP-02** | **Physical Worker Touchpoint** | A designated physical desk or partner kiosk (CSC / ITI / Trade Union office). |
| **SROP-03** | **Multilingual Voice/Text Bridge** | Native audio prompts in local vernacular languages (Assamese, Khasi, Bengali, etc.). |
| **SROP-04** | **Trade Guild Alignment** | Mutual non-adversarial understanding with local artisan and labor associations. |
| **SROP-05** | **7-Tier Supply Verification** | Verified breakdown from registered to discoverable, contactable, and active. |
| **SROP-06** | **Direct Zero-Fee Wage Rail** | Direct UPI/cash settlement protocol certified with zero platform deduction. |
| **SROP-07** | **48-Hour Grievance Desk** | Dedicated regional ombudsman capable of resolving disputes within 48 hours. |
| **SROP-08** | **Geofenced Telematics** | Verified perimeter checks (< 500m) for enterprise job site deployments. |
| **SROP-09** | **Tooling & Safety Equipment** | Verification of worker safety gear (helmets, harness, boots) where mandated. |
| **SROP-10** | **Secondary Field Auditor** | Independent local auditor on ground distinct from primary recruitment staff. |
| **SROP-11** | **Emergency Medical Protocol** | Documented tie-up with local district hospital / emergency ambulance service. |
| **SROP-12** | **Extreme Weather Protocol** | Automated system halt during severe flood / landslide red-alert warnings. |
