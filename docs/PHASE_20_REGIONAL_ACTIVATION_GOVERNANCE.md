### 1. The Regional Activation Lifecycle

Regional expansion in KAAMSETU follows an explicit 8-state governance lifecycle:

$$\text{research} \longrightarrow \text{candidate} \longrightarrow \text{readiness\_assessment} \longrightarrow \text{conditionally\_ready} \longrightarrow \text{controlled\_pilot} \longrightarrow \text{active} \longrightarrow \text{suspended} \longrightarrow \text{closed}$$

#### Mandatory Qualitative Gate Requirements:
To transition a region to `active`, the following 5 qualitative gates must be 100% satisfied regardless of quantitative user numbers:

1. **Physical Field Office Coordinator:** A physical human coordinator operating from an accessible local office.
2. **Institutional MoU:** Formal signed memorandum of understanding with the state skill development mission.
3. **Dual Administrator Approval:** Independent sign-offs by two distinct regional administrators.
4. **Emergency Worker Assistance Desk:** Operational multilingual hotline for on-site worker grievances.
5. **Zero Wage Commission Compliance:** Verified adherence to 0% wage cuts across all participating contractors.

### 3. Detailed Regional Case Studies

#### A. North Bengal / Siliguri Transit Corridor (`IN-WB-SIL`)
- **Quantitative Readiness:** 12 of 14 steps completed (85.7%). 150+ verified workers in pipeline.
- **Corridor Function:** Essential transit gateway connecting the North East states with West Bengal, Bihar, and Sikkim.
- **The Blocker:** Physical field office lease and local nodal coordinator remain unfinalized.
- **Governance Ruling:** In accordance with the Anti-Override Invariant, Siliguri's high score (85.7%) does **not** permit automated activation. Status remains **`pilot_candidate` / `conditionally_ready`** until the physical lease is executed.

#### B. Tripura State Expansion (`IN-TR`)
- **Quantitative Readiness:** 8 of 14 steps completed (57.1%).
- **The Blocker:** The Tripura Skill Development Mission (TSDM) institutional MoU is currently unratified. Local contractor liquidity is below threshold.
- **Governance Ruling:** Status strictly classified as **`gated_in_research`**. Live operations and contractor onboarding are prohibited.

### 4. Interstate Credential Portability Protocol

Workers registered in Assam or Meghalaya moving through the Siliguri corridor retain continuous credential validity under the interstate reciprocity protocol. State administrators cannot restrict worker mobility or invalidate credentials during interstate transit.
