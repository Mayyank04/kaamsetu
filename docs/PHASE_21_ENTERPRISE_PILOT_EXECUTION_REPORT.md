### 1. The Anti-Conflation Governance Framework

A recurring integrity risk in workforce software is the conflation of contractual intent with real-world physical and financial execution. KAAMSETU's enterprise architecture strictly enforces four ontological anti-conflation axioms:

> 1. **A signed contract does NOT equal worker deployment.**  
> 2. **A roster synchronization does NOT equal work performed.**  
> 3. **A deployment record does NOT equal wages paid.**  
> 4. **An invoice does NOT equal worker receipt.**  

To operationalize these invariants, Phase 21 expanded the enterprise state machine to an **11-stage sequential pipeline**:
$$\text{prospect} \rightarrow \text{due\_diligence} \rightarrow \text{draft\_agreement} \rightarrow \text{signed\_agreement} \rightarrow \text{pilot\_authorized} \rightarrow$$
$$\text{worker\_consent\_verified} \rightarrow \text{roster\_synced} \rightarrow \text{deployment\_confirmed} \rightarrow \text{work\_completed} \rightarrow \text{wage\_payment\_documented} \rightarrow \text{pilot\_closed}$$

### 5. Constitutional Invariant Defense & Commercial Monetization

Under KAAMSETU's financial model:
- **Worker Wages:** 100% of wages earned by deployed enterprise crew are paid directly to workers via direct bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS/UPI). Platform commission is **0.00%**.
- **Platform Licensing:** Enterprise clients pay a recurring B2B SaaS software licensing fee for attendance verification, credential verification, and GeM Form B audit compliance.
- **Taxation:** 18% GST is invoiced and remitted on all B2B SaaS fees, completely segregated from worker wage flows.
