## 1. Contractor Value Proposition

Contractors require rapid discovery of verified tradespeople, predictable daily wage rates, verified safety certifications, and clear compliance accounting for construction project staffing.

KAAMSETU Contractor Pro SaaS provides:
1. **Trade & Location Discovery:** Search across 22 recognized vocational trades and 36 States/UTs.
2. **Enterprise Workforce Rosters:** Manage multi-district crew rosters with real-time deployment statuses.
3. **Transparent Billing Architecture:** Complete breakdown showing 100% artisan wage disbursement, ₹0.00 platform commission, and 18% standard GST for enterprise invoices.
4. **Site Safety & Compliance Dossiers:** Inspect verified skill credentials, safety badges, and peer contractor endorsements before dispatch.

---

## 2. Contractor Dashboard Architecture

Located at `/contractor/dashboard`:
- **Header & Metric Strip:** Active site deployments, total roster size, scheduled projects, and verified compliance rate.
- **Roster Management:** View artisans assigned to current construction or maintenance contracts.
- **Booking & Project Request Pipeline:** Issue project invitations to artisans with proposed daily rates and project scopes.
- **Enterprise Invoice Generator:** Explicit mathematical breakdown separating base worker remuneration from GST.
