## 1. Institutional Role & Mission

Institutional partners train thousands of vocational candidates across trades but struggle with post-training placement tracking and long-term career outcomes.

KAAMSETU enables institutions to:
1. **Create and Monitor Cohorts:** Group trainees by trade, batch year, and training center.
2. **Endorse Trainee Profiles:** Stamp verified skill assessments directly onto the artisan's digital skill card without recurring administrative friction.
3. **Track Real-World Outcomes:** Privacy-preserving visibility into graduate employment rates, average daily earnings, and customer reviews.
4. **Strict Role-Based Security:** Institutional administration is strictly protected by server-side role validation (`requireInstitution()`). Ordinary users and unauthenticated guests are redirected with zero privilege leaks.
