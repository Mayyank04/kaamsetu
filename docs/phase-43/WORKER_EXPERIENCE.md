## 1. Principles of the Worker Experience

Skilled trade workers often face high digital literacy hurdles, low-end Android mobile devices with small screens, and unfamiliarity with complex enterprise web forms. The KAAMSETU Worker Experience adheres to four core principles:
1. **Dignity Over Commoditization:** The worker is presented as a skilled trade professional with deep hands-on expertise, not a gig worker or anonymous resource.
2. **Plain Language, Zero Jargon:** Every prompt, status badge, and instruction uses simple, direct, respectful terms. No technical or cryptographic obscurities.
3. **Step-by-Step Progress & Draft Preservation:** Account creation requires only phone or email plus password. Additional profile details (skills, work history, photo) can be saved as a draft and completed at any pace without losing work.
4. **Permanent Zero-Commission Entitlement:** Workers keep 100% of their earnings. Transparent payouts and transaction ledgers clearly show ₹0.00 platform commission on every job.

## 2. Guided 10-Section Workspace Architecture

The Worker Dashboard (`/dashboard`) is structured into 10 clear, navigable sections:

| Section Key | Navigation Label | Core Functionality & Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`dashboard`** | **Dashboard** | Personalized welcome, profile completion meter, public visibility toggle, skill summary, upcoming confirmed work, and clear next steps. |
| **`profile`** | **My Profile** | Comprehensive view of headline, bio, trade category, location, daily rate brackets, and contact information with inline edit modal. |
| **`skills`** | **My Skills** | Manage self-declared and verified trade proficiencies, tools, and years of experience with an intuitive add-skill workflow. |
| **`opportunities`** | **Work Opportunities** | Browse local contractor project openings, enterprise crew invitations, and community repair gigs. |
| **`bookings`** | **Bookings & Inquiries** | Real-time customer service inquiries with interactive status management (Confirm, Complete, Decline). |
| **`portfolio`** | **Portfolio** | Visual project showcase with photo uploads, project descriptions, client testimonials, and date stamps. |
| **`credentials`** | **Credentials & Trust** | Digital skill card preview, W3C verifiable credential parameters, scannable QR verification token, and KAAMSCORE™ breakdown. |
| **`earnings`** | **Earnings & Payouts** | Transparent payout ledger displaying completed jobs, direct bank/UPI disbursements, and 0% platform commission guarantee. |
| **`notifications`** | **Notifications** | Centralized activity feed for crew invitations, booking inquiries, verification milestones, and system updates. |
| **`settings`** | **Help & Settings** | Availability toggle, travel radius adjustment, language selector, grievance reporting, and secure sign-out. |
