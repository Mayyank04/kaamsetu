### 3. Banking UTR Audit & Zero Platform Intermediation

In accordance with KAAMSETU's constitutional architecture, the platform **never takes custody of worker wages**. Payments flow directly:
- **Peer-to-Peer UPI:** Direct transfer from customer VPA to worker VPA (e.g., `SBI-UPI-REF-202611-901823`).
- **Direct Cash on Completion:** Customer verifies cash handover via vernacular SMS OTP.
- **Enterprise Bank Transfer:** Contractor payroll direct NEFT/RTGS using scheduled commercial bank UTR (e.g., `SBIN-NEFT-UTR-20261115-90182348`).

**Zero Intermediary Escrow Cut:** Because funds flow directly between customer and worker, there is **zero escrow deduction, zero platform withholding, and zero payment gateway tolling on worker compensation**.
