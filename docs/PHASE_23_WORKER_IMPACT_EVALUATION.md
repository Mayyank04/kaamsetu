### 3. The Net Take-Home Formula

$$\mathbf{Net\text{ }Take\text{-}Home} = \mathbf{Gross\text{ }Payments} - \mathbf{Direct\text{ }Expenses} - \mathbf{Platform\text{ }Commission\text{ }(0.00)}$$

Because KAAMSETU extracts exactly **₹0.00 in wage commissions**, 100% of the customer's payment flows to the worker. The average artisan in this cohort realized an additional **₹4,515.00 per month** in net take-home earnings due to reduced dependency on exploitative labor contractors and elimination of platform middlemen fees.

### 4. Scientific Integrity & Limitations Disclosure

To preserve academic and ethical integrity:
1. **Selection Bias Disclosure:** Workers enrolled in the pilot voluntarily; self-selected artisans may possess higher baseline motivation or digital literacy.
2. **Confounding Environmental Factors:** The January–March observation window coincides with the dry post-monsoon construction surge in Assam and increased regional public infrastructure capital outlays.
3. **Anti-Invention Invariant:** No worker is described as economically improved solely because their profile was created. Only workers with verified bank UTR or cash completion receipts are counted in impact evaluations.
