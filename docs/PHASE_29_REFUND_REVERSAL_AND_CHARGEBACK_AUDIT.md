# KAAMSETU — PHASE 29 REFUND, REVERSAL & CHARGEBACK AUDIT

### 1. Worker Protection Against Customer Cancellations
- **Pre-Commencement Cancellation:** Customer payment returned; 0 worker liability or penalty recorded.
- **Post-Commencement Cancellation:** 100% of earned worker wage obligation preserved and funded via platform escrow; zero clawback from worker.
- **Partial Customer Refund:** Platform fee remains ₹0.00; worker compensation is never pro-rated downward.
- **Idempotent Webhooks:** Duplicate payment webhook deliveries detected via transaction idempotency key and rejected with zero duplicate ledger entries.
