### 1. The Institutional Integrity Principle

A Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) is not an operational partnership until executed; a meeting discussion is not an agreement; an unratified draft cannot authorize data sharing or joint worker mobilization.

KAAMSETU enforces an explicit **10-Status Institutional Review Lifecycle** within PostgreSQL table `public.institutional_instrument_reviews`.

---

### 2. The 10-Status Legal Instrument Taxonomy

```
[draft_stage] ──> [internal_review] ──> [counterparty_review] ──> [legal_clearance]
                                                                        │
[executed_active] <── [pending_execution] <─────────────────────────────┘
       │
       ├──> [amendment_pending] ──> [executed_active]
       ├──> [expired]
       └──> [terminated]

* [rejected] may occur from review or legal clearance.
```

1. **`draft_stage`**: Initial charter or draft MoU authored internally.
2. **`internal_review`**: Security, data privacy, and legal council approval within KAAMSETU.
3. **`counterparty_review`**: Draft formally transmitted to partner legal department.
4. **`legal_clearance`**: Both legal teams approve text without objections.
5. **`pending_execution`**: Clean final copies prepared for authorized signatory signing.
6. **`executed_active`**: Bilaterally signed, sealed, and entered into legal force.
7. **`amendment_pending`**: Formal addendum or scope modification under review.
8. **`expired`**: Instrument term has elapsed without renewal.
9. **`terminated`**: Formally cancelled prior to term expiration.
10. **`rejected`**: Partner declined to execute terms.

### 4. Mandatory Constitutional Clauses in All Agreements

All KAAMSETU institutional instruments incorporate 4 non-negotiable legal protections:

1. **The Zero-Commission Clause:** *"KAAMSETU and the Partner mutually covenant that no commission, deduction, or transaction percentage shall ever be extracted from the wages earned by informal or skilled workers mobilized under this instrument."*
2. **Worker Sovereignty & DPDP 2023 Compliance:** *"Workers retain sole ownership of their digital credentials and may revoke platform discoverability or export their verifiable credentials at any time without forfeiture of verified skill history."*
3. **Zero Financial Liability on State Treasury:** *"This collaboration imposes no financial obligation, grant guarantee, or procurement expenditure upon the State Government or its designated missions."*
4. **Anti-Exclusivity Covenant:** *"This agreement is strictly non-exclusive and does not prevent the State or the platform from engaging with other digital public infrastructure initiatives or training partners."*
