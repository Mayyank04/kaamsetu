## 2. Tax Classification & Segregation (Phase 28 Domain 22)

As tested in [TEST 94] and [TEST 95] of `test:phase28`:
- **Software Revenue:** Invoiced under GST Service Accounting Code **SAC 998313** ("Information technology design and development services") subject to 18% GST (CGST 9% + SGST 9%).
- **Worker Wage Pass-Through:** Handled as an unorganized labor reimbursement pass-through. No GST is levied on worker wages, and no GST input credits are offset against worker earnings.
- **Zero Infiltration:** Enterprise subscription billing and worker wage payouts operate on separate bank accounts and separate general ledger accounts.

---

## 3. Worker Zero-Extraction Guarantee

KAAMSETU explicitly audits that:
1. **Zero Registration / Onboarding Fees:** Free forever.
2. **Zero Bidding / Lead Generation Fees:** Artisans never pay to bid on jobs.
3. **Zero Inactive Account Maintenance Fees:** Balances never incur decay or penalty charges.
4. **Zero Dispute Filing Fees:** Access to ombudsperson tribunals is completely free of charge.
