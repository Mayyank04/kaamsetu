### 1. The Four Foundational Pillars of Worker Sovereignty

KAAMSETU is architected around four constitutional invariants to protect informal artisans from digital exploitation:
1. **Unilateral Identity Ownership**: Workers own their credentials and may export or delete them at any time.
2. **Zero Barrier Entry**: Zero degree, diploma, resume, smartphone, or DigiLocker requirement to register and work.
3. **Algorithmic Demographic Neutrality**: Zero discrimination based on caste, religion, gender, or state of origin.
4. **Strict AI Non-Invention**: The AI extraction pipeline preserves missing or unstated facts as `null`. It never fabricates experience, tools, or rates.

### 2. Independent Audit Findings

#### A. Open JSON Schema 2.0 Export (`VERIFIED`)
- Workers can execute a unilateral export of their complete professional dossier formatted in W3C-aligned open JSON Schema 2.0 (Test 89). The export includes verified skill claims, KaamScore, and work history.

#### B. Universal Consent Revocation (`VERIFIED`)
- Workers retain a one-click universal kill-switch. When a worker revokes platform or enterprise access, all active integration tokens and enterprise grants are invalidated immediately across all third parties (Test 90).

#### C. Inclusive Onboarding Without Degree Mandates (`VERIFIED`)
- Basic identity registration operates with zero educational credentials or formal degrees requested or required (Test 91). Informal skills (e.g. bricklaying, pipe fitting) are verified through peer endorsements and photo/video work samples, not academic degrees.

#### D. AI Non-Invention: Wage Rate Preservation (`VERIFIED`)
- In voice onboarding interviews, if a worker does not state a daily wage rate, the extraction pipeline leaves `statedDailyRate: null` (Test 92). The system strictly avoids defaulting to an arbitrary rate or minimum wage.

#### E. AI Non-Invention: Skill & Tool Accuracy (`VERIFIED`)
- In audio transcripts where an electrician mentions only "house wiring and fan installation", advanced or industrial skills (e.g., "solar inverter repair", "substation maintenance") are never hallucinated or injected (Test 93).

#### F. Algorithmic Demographic Neutrality in Match Ranking (`VERIFIED`)
- The matching algorithm uses exclusively trade-relevant features:
  - Trade relevance score
  - Verified experience hours
  - Geographic proximity (km)
  - Peer endorsement score
- Protected demographic characteristics (caste, religion, gender, ethnic origin, age) are strictly absent from matching and ranking feature vectors (Test 94).
