## 1. Context and Problem Statement

KAAMSETU has successfully established evidence-backed commercial and operational validation across two states in Northeast India:
- **Assam (`IN-AS`):** Guwahati & Kamrup Metropolitan District (600 active workers, 3 paying contractors, ASDM + Don Bosco partnerships).
- **Meghalaya (`IN-ML`):** Shillong & East Khasi Hills District (140 active workers, 2 paying contractors, Shillong Polytechnic partnership).

Three additional candidate corridors have been identified for potential future expansion:
1. **Tripura (`IN-TR`):** Agartala & West Tripura District.
2. **North Bengal (`IN-WB`):** Siliguri & Jalpaiguri Industrial Belt.
3. **Nagaland (`IN-NL`):** Dimapur Commercial Hub & Kohima.

**The Question:**  
Should KAAMSETU activate commercial operations and launch live worker matching in any of these three candidate corridors during Phase 15?

---

## 2. Objective Territorial Launch Gating Criteria

Under KAAMSETU's Governance Charter, no geographic territory may transition from `candidate` / `research` to `active` commercial operations unless it meets **ALL 5 Mandatory Launch Gates**:

| Launch Gate | Requirement | Gate Standard |
|---|---|---|
| **Gate 1: Institutional Anchor** | Signed Legal MoU | Executed bilateral MoU with State Skill Mission or accredited ITI |
| **Gate 2: Worker Seed Liquidity** | Verified Worker Supply | Minimum 100 verified workers registered with NSQF credentials |
| **Gate 3: Contractor Commercial Demand** | Paid B2B Intent | At least 2 local contractor organizations with paid SaaS tier |
| **Gate 4: Field Operations Node** | Physical Ground Presence | Designated, trained local Field Coordinator with territorial assignment |
| **Gate 5: Dual Administrative Sign-off** | Executive Approval | Unanimous approval from CTO and Regional Operations Director |

## 4. Formal Executive Decision

### DECISION:
**MAINTAIN CANDIDATE CORRIDORS IN `RESEARCH` STATUS. DO NOT ACTIVATE COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS OUTSIDE ASSAM AND MEGHALAYA AT THIS TIME.**

### Rationale:
1. **Capital and Focus Preservation:** Activating operations prematurely without physical coordinators or institutional anchors dilutes regional liquidity in Assam and Meghalaya, degrades response times for worker grievances, and risks contractor churn.
2. **Integrity Invariant:** KAAMSETU's non-negotiable principle is never to fabricate users, contracts, or regional launches. The platform must only declare regions active when real-world ground operations exist.
3. **Data Separation:** As verified in Phase 15 Test 32, candidate region records remain strictly segregated from operational production analytics.
