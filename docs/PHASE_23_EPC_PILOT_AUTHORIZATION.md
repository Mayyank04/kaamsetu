### 1. The Enterprise Pilot Integrity Problem

In the enterprise workforce technology sector, software platforms routinely present imported spreadsheet rosters or letters of intent as "live enterprise workforce deployments."

KAAMSETU enforces the **Enterprise Anti-Conflation Framework**:
$$\mathbf{Roster\text{ }Sync} \ne \mathbf{Worker\text{ }Deployment}$$
$$\mathbf{Project\text{ }Record} \ne \mathbf{Employment\text{ }/\text{ }Wage\text{ }Disbursement}$$

No pilot engagement may transition to `authorized_active` status without satisfying all 10 mandatory authorization gates.

### 2. The 10-Requirement Pilot Authorization Gate

| Gate # | Authorization Requirement | Conformance Standard | System Gate Enforcement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gate 1** | **Verified Legal Counterparty** | Legal entity registered in MCA / state registry | Automated CIN / GSTIN validation |
| **Gate 2** | **Authorized Signatory** | Corporate Board Resolution or Power of Attorney | Signatory authority documentation audit |
| **Gate 3** | **Executed Bilateral Agreement** | Wet-ink stamped agreement with execution ref | Locked if draft or under review |
| **Gate 4** | **Approved Project Scope** | Detailed technical scope, job roles & shift specs | Minimum 15-character audited scope |
| **Gate 5** | **Worker Protection Covenants** | Non-negotiable 0.00% wage commission clause | Mandatory contractual covenant |
| **Gate 6** | **Wage Payment Responsibilities** | Explicit contractor commitment to bank UTR wage rail | Direct worker payment obligation |
| **Gate 7** | **Insurance & Safety Obligations** | Mandatory PPE gear & site accident insurance | On-site safety compliance check |
| **Gate 8** | **Data-Sharing & Privacy Terms** | DPDP 2023 purpose limitation & consent terms | Purpose-bound enterprise roster access |
| **Gate 9** | **Individual Worker Consent** | Explicit consent from each deployed worker | Roster sync rejected if consent = 0 |
| **Gate 10**| **Authorized Deployment Record** | Verified geofenced check-in (< 500m) record | Deployment locked until first check-in |
