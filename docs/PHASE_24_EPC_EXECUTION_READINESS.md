### 1. Enterprise EPC Pilot Context

To bridge informal artisans to high-value national infrastructure civil projects, KAAMSETU has developed structured workforce deployment pilots for:
1. **NHIDCL Assam Highway 27 Expansion:** Bridge carpentry, shuttering masonry, and structural rebar cohorts.
2. **Shillong Airport Civil Infrastructure Project:** Terminal joinery, interior carpentry, and architectural finishes.

Because civil construction sites involve severe physical hazards and multi-tier sub-contracting risks, worker safety and prompt wage payment cannot be left to informal verbal trust.

### 3. Detailed Audit of Gate 3 (`BLOCKER-03`)

- **Underlying Instrument:** `EPC/NHIDCL-SHL/PILOT-2026-REV3`
- **Parties:** KAAMSETU (Platform), Prime EPC Contractor (Joint Venture Lead), Sub-contractor (Site Operator).
- **Core Mandatory Covenants:**
  1. **Direct Wage Escrow:** Prime EPC contractor must deposit full 30-day cohort wage reserves into an escrow account operated under joint instruction with KAAMSETU.
  2. **Zero Deduction Warranty:** Contractor explicitly covenants that workers will receive 100% of agreed statutory wages with ₹0.00 deducted for tool rentals, safety boots, transport, or administrative commissions.
  3. **Insurance Cover:** Every deployed artisan covered by a minimum ₹5,00,000 accidental life and disability insurance policy funded entirely by the contractor.
  4. **Immediate Withdrawal Right:** If safety standards or wage schedules are violated, KAAMSETU retains the right to immediately withdraw the artisan cohort without contractor financial penalty.
- **Current Legal Status:** Tripartite agreement draft Revision 3 has been agreed in substance; currently awaiting formal Board Resolution and wet-ink execution on non-judicial stamp paper from consortium partners.

### 4. Statutory GeM Form B Compliance

To ensure complete harmony with Indian public procurement rules:
- KAAMSETU automatically compiles daily geofenced muster rolls into standard **Government e-Marketplace (GeM) Form B** format.
- Form B registers document daily artisan check-in times, task categories, wage disbursement records, and bank transaction reference numbers (UTRs).
- Eliminates ghost workers and contractor attendance manipulation.
