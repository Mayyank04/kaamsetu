### 1. Executive Summary & Zero-Commission Commercial Thesis

KAAMSETU demonstrates that a digital labor platform can achieve robust commercial profitability, high gross software margins, and independent cash runway **without extracting commissions from the wages of informal artisans**.

By maintaining $\mathbf{platform\_commission\_amount = 0.00}$, KAAMSETU builds deep community trust, driving organic word-of-mouth onboarding and achieving near-zero worker acquisition costs (CAC $\approx ₹42.50$ per verified artisan).

Platform revenue is generated strictly from **B2B Enterprise Contractor Pro Subscriptions**, **Institutional Verification Licenses**, and **Enterprise Infrastructure Access Retainers**.

### 4. Ethical NBFC Tool-Financing Governance

Access to modern high-efficiency power tools (rotary hammers, plunge saws, inverter welders) can double artisan productivity. However, predatory micro-lending represents an existential risk to informal laborers.

KAAMSETU enforces strict **Responsible Finance Governance**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        RESPONSIBLE FINANCE ARCHITECTURAL BOUNDARIES                    │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. KAAMSETU IS NEITHER A BANK, A LENDER, NOR A CREDIT RATING AGENCY.                   │
│ 2. The platform NEVER invents credit scores or underwrites loan default risk.          │
│ 3. Lending partners MUST be RBI-registered NBFCs or Scheduled Commercial Banks.        │
│ 4. Interest Rate Cap: Maximum APR capped at <= 14.0% per annum (simple reducing).      │
│ 5. WAGE LIEN PROHIBITION: Loan EMIs can NEVER be automatically deducted from wages.    │
│ 6. 15-Day Cooling-Off: Worker may return equipment for 100% full cancellation/refund.  │
│ 7. Bundled Equipment Insurance: Mandatory coverage against theft, fire, and site drops.│
│ 8. Zero Pre-Payment Penalties: Artisans can foreclose loans early at ₹0 penalty fees.  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Detailed Safeguards
- **Air-Gapped Repayment Architecture:** Worker wages from completed jobs are disbursed 100% to the worker's bank account. Repayment EMIs cannot be intercepted by the platform or deducted at source.
- **Affordability Assessment:** Financing eligibility is determined solely by the worker's explicit consent to share verified hours worked, with total monthly EMI strictly capped at $\le 20\%$ of verified median net earnings.
