# KAAMSETU — CONTRACTOR CUSTOMER DISCOVERY INTERVIEW GUIDE
## Standardized Protocol for 15–20 B2B Contractor Validation Interviews

---

## 1. Interview Objective & Core Hypotheses

The objective of customer discovery is to rigorously determine whether commercial contractors (electrical, plumbing, construction, interior, and facility management) have a genuine willingness to pay for KAAMSETU's Contractor Pro SaaS.

### Core Hypotheses Under Test:
1. **The Verification Deficit Hypothesis**: Contractors waste 4–8 hours per week attempting to verify informal workers' skill claims through informal trial-and-error, leading to costly site rework.
2. **The Compliance Bottleneck Hypothesis**: Commercial clients (corporate offices, residential societies, government PWD/DISCOM tenders) demand verified rosters with ID proofs, which contractors struggle to produce manually.
3. **The Willingness-to-Pay (WTP) Hypothesis**: Contractors are willing to pay ₹999/month (Pro - 25 workers) or ₹2,999/month (Business - 100 workers) for automated crew compliance dossiers, multi-project allocation, and cryptographic work record signing, provided the platform extracts 0% from workers.

Interviews must cover 15–20 real contractors across six distinct sectors in the Guwahati / Kamrup Metropolitan region:

## 3. Semi-Structured Interview Protocol (45 Minutes)

### Section 1: Current Operating Reality (10 mins)
1. "Walk me through how you recruited your current crew of workers."
2. "When a new worker shows up at your site or a Naka chowk claiming 8 years of experience, how do you verify that claim today?"
3. "How many days on average does it take to find out whether a worker actually knows what they claimed?"
4. "What is the financial cost of a worker who turned out to be unqualified on a live project?"

### Section 2: Crew Records & Client Compliance (10 mins)
1. "How do you maintain records of who worked on which site last month? (Paper diary, WhatsApp groups, Excel, mental record?)"
2. "Have your clients (homeowners, builders, corporate facility managers) ever asked for proof of worker qualifications, Aadhaar, or police verification?"
3. "What happens when a client disputes billing hours or claims defective workmanship?"

### Section 3: Value Proposition & Software Expenditure (15 mins)
1. "What software tools do you currently pay for in your contracting business? (Accounting such as Tally/Vyapar, CAD, Attendance apps like PagarBook/Khatabook, none?)"
2. *Show Contractor Pro live dashboard*: "If you could pull up a verified crew dossier with Aadhaar verification badges, civic experience tiers, and one-click PDF export for clients—how would that affect your operations?"
3. "KAAMSETU charges 0% commission on worker wages. Workers' digital identity is permanently free. The contractor pays ₹999/month for up to 25 workers or ₹2,999/month for up to 100 workers. What is your immediate reaction to that price?"
4. "What single feature would make this an instant 'must-buy' for your next site contract?"

### Section 4: Objections, Decision-Making & Pilot Commitment (10 mins)
1. "What would prevent you from using this platform tomorrow?"
2. "Who in your firm makes the decision to purchase software?"
3. "Would you be willing to test Contractor Pro on your next live project with a 14-day zero-risk trial?"

## 4. Evaluation Rubric & Scoring Criteria

For each interview, record quantitative ratings (1 to 5):
- **Pain Severity (1–5)**: How critical is worker unreliability to their business? (1 = trivial annoyance, 5 = severe business risk / margin killer).
- **Current Spend Appetite (1–5)**: Existing software/business tool budget.
- **WTP Validation (Yes / Neutral / No)**: Clear affirmative response to proposed ₹999/mo or ₹2,999/mo tiers.
- **Commitment Level**:
  - Level 0: Uninterested.
  - Level 1: Polite interest, no action.
  - Level 2: Agrees to join beta/trial.
  - Level 3: Agrees to sign up for paid pilot.
