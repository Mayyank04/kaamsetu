### 1. The Regulated Entity Boundary

Informal artisans are routinely preyed upon by predatory digital lenders using hidden fees, extortionate APRs, and aggressive physical recovery agents.

KAAMSETU enforces three foundational legal and ethical boundaries:
1. **KAAMSETU is NOT a Lender:** The platform does not hold a banking or NBFC license, does not take deposits, and does not disburse loans from its balance sheet.
2. **Zero Algorithmic Credit Scoring:** KAAMSETU strictly refuses to assign a "credit score" or creditworthiness grade to informal workers based on their identity or work records.
3. **Zero Pre-Approval Marketing:** Misleading marketing banners (e.g., "Pre-Approved Loan for ₹50,000") are strictly banned across all worker interfaces.

---

### 2. Tripartite LSP Architecture (RBI Compliant)

All proposed equipment and tool micro-financing operates strictly under the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Digital Lending Guidelines (2022)** via a formal Tripartite Structure:

```
[Borrower (Skilled Artisan)]  <──>  [RBI-Regulated NBFC Partner]
            │                                    │
            │ (Consent & Work History)           │ (Underwriting & Credit Risk)
            ▼                                    ▼
   [KAAMSETU (LSP Node)]  <─────────>  [Scheduled Commercial Bank]
   - 0.00% Platform Cut                - Direct Escrow Disbursement
   - Verifiable Credentials            - Zero Platform Commingling
```

### 3. Non-Negotiable Worker Protection Covenants

Before any NBFC partner agreement can be signed, the partner must contractually agree to 6 non-negotiable covenants:

| Covenant | Worker Protection Standard | Regulatory / Audit Enforcement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Transparent KFS** | Key Fact Statement (KFS) provided before loan execution | Mandates all-inclusive APR, fee breakdown, and schedule |
| **APR Negotiation Target**| Proposed 11.5% APR strictly qualified as *negotiation target* | Prohibits deceptive "fixed low-rate" marketing |
| **Zero Platform Cut** | Zero commission or origination cut extracted from loan | **Constitutional Invariant: strictly ₹0.00** |
| **Statutory Cooling-Off**| Minimum 3 business days right to exit without penalty | Worker can return funds with zero exit penalty |
| **Non-Recourse Protection**| Strict ban on wage attachment or tool seizure | Lender cannot seize essential livelihood tools |
| **Zero Harassment Desk**| Dedicated 48-hour RBI ombudsman grievance rail | Immediate suspension of recovery actions upon dispute |
