# KAAMSETU — PHASE 29 NON-RETALIATION ADVERSARIAL TEST REPORT

### 1. Anti-Retaliation Controls
1. **Review Quarantining:** Employer reviews submitted during active disputes are quarantined and hidden from worker profiles.
2. **Unilateral Closure Prevention:** Employers cannot close or dismiss dispute records without ombudsperson or worker consent.
3. **Tribunal Non-Retaliation Trigger:** Trigger `trg_dispute_appeal_non_retaliation` enforces active protection status during pending appeals.
