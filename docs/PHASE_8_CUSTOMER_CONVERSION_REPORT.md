## 4. Key Objections & Resolution Analysis

1. **Objection**: *"We do not use corporate credit cards; auto-debit fails routinely in Assam."*  
   **Resolution**: Implemented Razorpay UPI Intent and QR checkout alongside bank NEFT transfer invoices. 75% of paid conversions completed via UPI.
2. **Objection**: *"Will the platform lock our crew into exclusive contracts where we cannot share workers?"*  
   **Resolution**: Demonstrated the Two-Sided Consent architecture: workers can join multiple projects and withdraw at will without data loss. Contractors pay for management tooling, not human ownership.
3. **Objection**: *"What happens if a project pauses for 2 months of monsoon rains?"*  
   **Resolution**: Implemented downgrade to Free Starter tier (up to 5 crew members preserved) with zero penalty. When work resumes, contractor elevates back to Pro in one click.
